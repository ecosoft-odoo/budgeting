This module defines the Budget Period (``budget.period``), the time
frame shared by budget applications such as budget proposals and budget
control.

A period has a date range, the companies it applies to (empty: all
companies) and a scope:

- **Fiscal Period**: selected by document date. Fiscal periods of the
  same companies (a company and its branches included) cannot overlap,
  so a date always belongs to one fiscal period per company.
- **Lifetime**: a multi-year budget of one analytic account, which may
  overlap fiscal periods. Lifetime periods are managed by Budget
  Control.

Budget periods can be configured under Settings > Technical > Budget >
Budget Periods. Each budget application can also add the period to its
own configuration menu and add its own settings to the period.
