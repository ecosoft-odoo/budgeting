Budget Plan

This module allocates the budget of a fiscal budget period to analytic accounts with a Budget Plan, and creates or updates their Budget Control Sheets from it.

Budget Control Sheets created or adopted by a Budget Plan keep a direct link to that plan. This makes ownership of the allocated amount explicit and prevents another plan from taking over the same active sheet. Once the module is installed, Budget Control Sheets are no longer created directly from their menu; Lifetime budgets are still set up from their analytic account.

Upgrading budget_control from an earlier version installs this module, which keeps the existing plans.

Important

This is an alpha version, the data model and design can change at any time without warning. Only for development or testing purpose, do not use in production. More details on development status

Usage

  1. Menu > Budgeting > Budget Planning > Budget Plan, create a plan for a fiscal Budget Period.
  2. Add the analytic accounts with Get All Analytic Accounts or by hand, and enter their New Budget. The Forward Balance of the period is added to the Allocated amount. Confirming the plan adds the yearly analytics of its period, and the analytics without Budget Period open in it that are dated or had a Budget Control Sheet the period before.
  3. Confirm the plan, then click Create/Update Budget Control to create the Budget Control Sheets or update their Allocated amount.

A plan line can be allocated by dimension with its Details button, e.g. Department 1 gets Fund A 100 and Fund B 50 (accounts of plans without Control Budget): they allocate its New Budget and Forward Balance, the New Budget being their sum less the forward balance, and its Budget Control Sheet stays one for the analytic account. A forward balance completed after them is left Unallocated: the plan is confirmed, and its sheets created or updated, once the details allocate it. A detail may leave out the account of a plan, e.g. Fund A and Fund A / Type 1: documents match the detail with exactly their accounts, Fund A without a type or Fund A / Type 1, unless the plan is mandatory in budget plans (its default applicability, or an applicability for the Budget Plan domain): each detail then needs one. With Control Allocation of the Budget Period (ticked by default), the budget check then blocks documents whose analytic rows charge an allocation that does not exist, or that the commitments and actuals of the period exceed. Untick it to allocate by dimension for information only. The Allocations page of the sheet shows them with their Consumed and Available amounts. While the period controls them, the details must cover the commitments and actuals of the period by their dimensions, also when the plan is set to draft to revise them: an account cannot be added to the only detail of a consumed combination, nor can all the details be removed once the analytic account has been consumed. Commitments carried forward to a period, with their Forward Dimensions, must fit its allocations too.

Development

  • budget.plan._prepare_budget_control_sheet() gives the values of the sheets a plan creates.
  • budget.control._get_budget_allocations() gives the details of the plan managing a sheet, for its analytic account.
  • budget.plan.line._get_budget_mandatory_fnames() gives the plan columns each detail of a line needs.
  • budget.plan.line.detail._get_budget_consumptions(budget_period, analytic, fnames) sums the commitments and actuals of an analytic account by dimension, and _check_budget_allocation(budget_period, budget_moves) returns the messages of the allocation check, run on the budget moves of budget.period._get_control_budget_moves().
  • budget.commit.forward._check_forward_allocation() runs the check on the commitments a forward carries to its period.
  • budget.plan._get_eligible_analytic_domain() gives the analytics a plan takes when updated, and budget.plan.line._check_details_allocated() checks that the details of a line allocate its New Budget and Forward Balance.
